    How this works

    Your daily weight bounces around with water, salt and digestion — noise. The trend is a 10% exponentially-smoothed moving average that filters out the noise so you can see what your body is actually doing:

    trendₕ = trendₕ₋₁ + 0.1 × (weightₕ − trendₕ₋₁)

    A pound of fat is about 3500 kcal, so the rate your trend moves tells you your average daily calorie surplus or deficit. Watch the trend, not the scale.
